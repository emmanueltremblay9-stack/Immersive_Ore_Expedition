Tiny Vertical Mine Entrance
Recognizing the descent
"A hole in the ground is rarely interesting. The reason someone dug it usually is."
<br><br>
A stone rim, oak posts and planks, and a raised lantern mark this small IOE entrance. Examine the opening and the route below rather than treating the light as a resource indicator.
<br><br>
The entrance belongs to the connected expedition-site layout. Inspect the descent, preserve a way back and follow the accessible passages before deciding what can be recovered.
<br><br>
An entrance is evidence of an excavation, not proof of a rich or productive deposit. A site can be dry; the visible frame does not identify its hidden resource or quality.
<br><br>
