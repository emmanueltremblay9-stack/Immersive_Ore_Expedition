Prospector Camp
An excavation worth investigating
"Their tools were left behind. Their survey notes were not."
<br><br>
IOE prospector camps combine a worked surface area with access to an excavation. Depending on the layout and condition, a camp may show a fire site, wooden supports, worktables, storage containers and a host-rock outcrop.
<br><br>
Some camps look recently used; others are abandoned or damaged. Their condition and furnishings vary. Neither a container nor a weathered shelter guarantees valuable ore, a survey document or a productive reserve.
<br><br>
The outcrop is part of the camp’s composition, not an independent promise of extractable mineral blocks. Follow actual access and examine the underground formation before assessing the site.
<br><br>
A camp is an invitation to investigate. Its appearance alone does not identify the resource, establish the remaining IE reserve or disclose a verified quality.
