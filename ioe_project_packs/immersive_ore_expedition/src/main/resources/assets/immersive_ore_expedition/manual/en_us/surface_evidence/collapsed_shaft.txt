Collapsed Mine Shaft
Reading a damaged rim
"The earth eventually takes back every tunnel. Fortunately, it is not particularly good at hiding where they used to be."
<br><br>
Look for a stone rim disturbed by gravel and tuff, with an uneven wooden support and patches of mossy cobblestone. These are the placed surface details of IOE’s collapsed-shaft variant.
<br><br>
Inspect the opening and surrounding ground before descending. The damaged appearance describes the structure; it does not promise an automatic collapse, a special excavation challenge or a second hidden route.
<br><br>
This surface clue is part of a connected expedition site. It does not guarantee a productive chamber, identify a mineral family or reveal the site’s quality.
<br><br>
Judge the passages and formation you actually encounter, and maintain a reliable return route.
