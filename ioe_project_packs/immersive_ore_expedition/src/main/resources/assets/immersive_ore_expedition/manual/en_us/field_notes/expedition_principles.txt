Expedition Principles
Reading the land
"A miner asks where the ore is. An engineer asks why it is there."
<br><br>
A successful expedition begins with observation. Valuable minerals are not distributed equally: a promising region is a reason to investigate, not a guarantee of a productive site.
<br><br>
Use the evidence you actually encounter. Establish a safe return route, examine the formation and identify what can be recovered before committing extraction equipment.
<br><br>
Keep three questions separate: where is the formation, what resource has been identified, and what has been established about its quality? An entrance or a surface clue alone does not answer all three.
<br><br>
Reading this guide does not discover a site or unlock recipes. Native crafting and industrial progression remain available independently of these pages, according to their own rules.
<br><br>
This field guide explains general principles. It supplies no hidden deposit locations or individual survey results.
