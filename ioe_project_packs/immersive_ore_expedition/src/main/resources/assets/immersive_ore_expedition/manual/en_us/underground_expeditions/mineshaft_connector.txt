Mineshaft Connector
Following the constructed route
"The shortest route is not always the safest. Whoever built these supports understood that."
<br><br>
An IOE surface site connects to an underground chamber through a shaft and a supported passage. Look for ladders, oak supports and plank flooring as you follow the constructed access below the surface clue.
<br><br>
Inspect junctions before moving on. A short gravel-ended branch is part of the connected layout; it is not a promise of another deposit. Keep track of the route you used and preserve a way back to the surface.
<br><br>
Bring lighting and ordinary exploration supplies. Supports and ladders are placed blocks, not a guarantee against hazards introduced by the surrounding world or later changes to the site.
<br><br>
The connector is an access component of the expedition site, not a separate resource discovery. Follow the passage to inspect the actual formation before planning extraction. Reading this page does not advance your personal Discovery Journal.
